Characters in the game Cyberpunk 2077

V (Main Character/Playable Character)
Born in 2054
Main Character Played by the user, they can either be male or female and can be customized very thoroughly.
Lives in City Centre after Tutorial.
Johnny Silverhand
Born in 1988
Ex Rock-star turned Terrorist against Multinational Organizations, he thinks that the Corporations are only trying to control everyone.
Lived across many Domiciles in Night City.
Jackie Welles
Born in 2046
V's Trusted Friend and Associate in the Mercenary Business, Mother is Mama Welles.
Lives in Heywood, behind El Coyote Cojo, their Bar.
Solomon Reed (Played by Idris Elba)
At least 100 years, old No Specified Date of Birth
Works for the NUSA FIA (Federal Intelligence Agency), Meets V in the Badlands after rescuing the President of the NUSA.
No Specified Domicile
Yorinobu Arasaka
Born in 1995
Heir to the Arasaka Corporation, his Father is Saburo Arasaka. He rebels against his Father and partakes in gang leadership.
Lives in the Very Top Penthouse Suite at Arasaka Tower in Night City.
